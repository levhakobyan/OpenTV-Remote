### 2026-10-26 — Bluetooth HID registration lost after pairing/settings

User confirmed that TV/phone Bluetooth bonding succeeds, but Luma fails to connect with “HID registration ended.” Android's official [BluetoothHidDevice documentation](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/bluetooth/BluetoothHidDevice) states that registration ends when the app leaves foreground; a paired host and an acquired profile proxy do not establish a registered HID app. Inspected the actual connect branch: after registration ended, `hid != null` and `registered == false` caused it to skip both registration and connection, waiting until timeout. This is a concrete local recovery defect, not evidence of a TV box rejecting mouse reports.

The provider now re-registers whenever registration is absent, replaces stale proxies, invalidates late callbacks before releasing the old profile, and reports registration loss separately from TV compatibility. Setup explicitly releases HID in background and restores it on return. Resume keeps the active Bluetooth setup sheet instead of reconnecting the previous TV and disposing its setup owner. Bonding records are preserved; there is no background service or automatic mouse-to-keyboard mode downgrade.
